01

Philosophy & Overview

Welcome to Word Explorer Offline. We believe that playing word puzzle games should be a relaxing, enjoyable, and completely private experience. Your privacy is paramount, and this Privacy Policy explains how our application functions without collecting, transmitting, or sharing any personal data.

Unlike modern games that require active internet connections, cloud accounts, or advertising IDs, Word Explorer Offline is designed from the ground up as a fully self-contained offline application.

02

To eliminate any ambiguity, we explicitly state that Word Explorer Offline does NOT collect, store, transmit, or process any of the following categories of data:

✕
Personal Identifiers No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials.
✕

✕
Contacts & Media No access to your photo library, camera, contacts, or internal storage files.
03

Local Device Storage

In order for Word Explorer Offline to save your game progress and remember your settings between play sessions, the app saves small data structures strictly on your local device hardware.

This includes:

  • Puzzle Progression: Completed levels, unlocked word packs, and current grid states.
  • Game Preferences: Audio settings (sound effects/music toggles), selected dictionary language, and dark/light UI theme choices.
  • Player Achievements & Scores: High scores, solved word streaks, and star totals.

Technical note: On Web browsers, data is stored in standard localStorage. On iOS and Android, data is stored inside isolated app preference containers (SharedPreferences / UserDefaults). This data never leaves your device.

04

Device Permissions

Word Explorer Offline requests zero sensitive device permissions.

Zero Network Permission Needed: The app manifest does not include internet access permissions (android.permission.INTERNET is omitted). This ensures at the operating system level that the app cannot communicate with external servers even if connected to Wi-Fi or cellular data.
05

Children's Privacy & Family Safety

Word Explorer Offline is designed to be family-friendly and completely safe for players of all ages, including young children.

We comply fully with the United States Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) regarding children, and the Apple App Store Kids Category requirements.

Because no personal data is ever collected or requested from any user, parents and guardians can rest assured that children can play without exposure to behavioral tracking, profile building, or third-party interactions.

06

Third-Party Services & SDKs

Many games integrate third-party Software Development Kits (SDKs) for analytics, crash reporting, or ad serving. Word Explorer Offline contains NO third-party SDKs.

  • No Ad Networks: No Google AdMob, Unity Ads, AppLovin, or banner advertisement integrations.
  • No Telemetry / Analytics: No Firebase Analytics, GameAnalytics, or Mixpanel integration.
  • No Social Media Plugins: No Facebook SDK or social login tracking.
07

Security & User Data Control

Since all data is stored locally on your physical device, the security of your game progress is protected by your device's built-in sandbox security model.

How to Delete Your Data: You retain complete control over all stored data. You can erase all save states, high scores, and preferences at any time by simply selecting "Clear App Data" in your device system settings or by uninstalling the application.
